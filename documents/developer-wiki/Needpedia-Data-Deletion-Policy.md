This policy outlines how users can request deletion of their personal data from Needpedia.
How to Request Data Deletion
Send an email to info@needpedia.org with the subject line "Data Deletion Request"
Include the email address associated with your Needpedia account
What Will Be Deleted
Upon request, we will delete:
Your entire user account


Note: Users can also delete their accounts directly through their account settings at any time.
Response Time
We will process all deletion requests within 10 business days
You will receive confirmation once your data has been deleted
For any questions about this policy, please contact info@needpedia.org
Last updated: December 2024
